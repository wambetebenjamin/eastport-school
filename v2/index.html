Let's talk about studying at Eastport

Eastport combines trade practice with management and technology in every programme, and our trainers are practising nurses, chefs, network engineers and accountants. The aim is simple: every graduate leaves with the hands-on competence, communication and problem-solving skills that Kericho employers are hiring for.

Classes run in three streams, attachment letters are processed by the placement office, and the bursary desk helps students apply for county and national funding.

  • TVETA registered · KNEC 518112 · CDACC 0350007
  • Three intakes a year — January, May and September
  • Fees payable in three instalments per term

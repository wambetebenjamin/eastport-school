Three simple steps

Join the January 2027 cohort

Apply online, then visit Chai Plaza 2nd Floor with your documents to confirm your place. Applications are reviewed within one working day.

  1. Complete this form and choose your programme.
  2. Our admissions team calls you to confirm your entry grade and fee plan.
  3. Pay a 40% deposit and collect your admission letter and reporting date.

Bring with you:

  • KCSE / KCPE result slip (original and copy)
  • National ID or birth certificate
  • Two passport photographs

Questions?
Call 0757 478 077 or WhatsApp the admissions desk. Office hours: Mon–Fri: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM · Sat: 8:30 AM – 1:00 PM.

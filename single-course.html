Programme overview

Master commercial kitchen production, front office service and beverage sales inside our working restaurant and pastry hub.

This programme combines classroom theory with supervised practical sessions, so you graduate with a portfolio of real work. Assessment follows the national competency framework, with both internal practical evaluations and the final examining body assessment.

Career pathways

Graduates progress into employment, self-employment or further study. Eastport alumni from this faculty are working across Kericho County and the wider South Rift.

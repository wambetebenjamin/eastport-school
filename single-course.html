Programme overview

Master commercial kitchen production, front office service and beverage sales inside our working restaurant and pastry hub.

This programme combines classroom theory with supervised production shifts, so you graduate with a portfolio of real service and kitchen work. Assessment follows the national competency framework, with both internal practical evaluations and the final examining body assessment.

Career pathways

Graduates progress into employment, self-employment or further study. Recent Eastport alumni from this faculty have opened catering units in Kericho CBD and joined tea estate resorts across the South Rift.

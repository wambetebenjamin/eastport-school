Who we are

Kericho’s hands-on TVET college

Eastport International Training College is a private TVET institution located on the second floor of Chai Plaza, Kenyatta Street, in the heart of Kericho CBD. We offer 253+ approved programmes across eleven faculties, examined by KNEC and assessed by CDACC, with international pathways through ABMA, ABE, ICM and ICDL.

Our promise is simple: at least 70% of your time is spent practising — in clinical simulation, computer suites, commercial kitchens and engineering workshops — supported by tutors who have worked in the industries they teach.

  • TVETA registered with KNEC and CDACC examination centres on campus
  • Guaranteed three-month attachment with 98% placement
  • Three-part termly fee instalments and bursary application support
  • Morning, afternoon and evening streams for working students

Why Eastport

Six reasons families choose us

Accreditation matters, but so does what happens in the laboratory on a Tuesday morning. Here is what you get in both.

Clinical & technical labs

Diagnostic centrifuges, sterile draw stations, network sandboxes and stainless-steel kitchens — not photographs of them.

Guaranteed attachment

Three-month industry attachment for every learner, with 98% placement across hospitals, hotels, garages and county offices.

Flexible fee plans

A 40% deposit followed by three instalments per term, with no learner locked out of laboratories or examinations.

Recognised certification

We are a KNEC (518112), CDACC (0350007) and TVETA centre — your certificate is examinable and verifiable.

Small, mentored classes

Tutors know every learner by name. Extra remedial sessions run free of charge before national examinations.

Kericho CBD campus

Second floor of Chai Plaza on Kenyatta Street — walking distance from matatu stages, banks and county offices.

Our vision

Skills that build the South Rift economy

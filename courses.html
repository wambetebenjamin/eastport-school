Fees & funding

Termly fees, paid in three parts

All fees quoted are per term and include tuition, laboratory or workshop consumables and internal examinations. A 40% deposit secures your place, with the balance payable in two further instalments. National examination fees (KNEC, CDACC, KASNEB) are paid separately.

  • Kericho County bursary application support at our welfare desk
  • HELB TVET loan and scholarship guidance for eligible students
  • Pay by M-PESA paybill, bank transfer or at the finance office

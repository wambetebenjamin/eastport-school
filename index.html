Who we are

Kericho's hands-on TVET college

Eastport International Training College is a private TVET institution registered by TVETA as centre 401002 and an examination centre for KNEC 518112 and CDACC 0350007. Learning here happens in working labs, kitchens and workshops — not just classrooms.

  • Clinical simulation, phlebotomy and computer labs on the same floor as your classroom
  • Guaranteed three-month attachment with our Kericho and county referral partners
  • Fees payable in three instalments per term, with a 40% deposit on acceptance

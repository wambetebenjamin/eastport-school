Two complete builds

Choose the design for eastportinternationalcollege.ac.ke

Both versions now share one design language — the crest's navy and gold, white cards and the same campus photography — and both portals use the same sign-in screen. They differ in structure: a brochure-style college website with a student portal, or a university-style site with an eLearning system. Open each one, use the portal, then tell us which letter to take forward.

Version A

College website

The college website on the approved template: navy top strip, white sticky navigation with course search and an apply CTA, a split hero with floating student statistics, a five-item trust strip, faculty category tabs, four-column course cards, a course search band and a campus photo wall — all leading into one shared student portal.

  • ●Split hero: the claim, accreditation facts and two calls to action on the left, a campus photograph with two floating statistic pills on the right. Interior pages repeat the pattern as a title-and-facts banner.
  • ●One login for every role: the student, staff and guardian tabs sit in the same sign-in card, on the same page shell, with demo credentials, password visibility and honest validation.
  • ●Ten pages: home, about, courses, course detail, news, contact, apply, login, dashboard, 404.
  • ●Campus photo wall, plus photography on the about, contact, courses and apply pages.

Version B

University website + eLearning

A university-style website with a full-width photo slider, programme levels and enquiry form, plus an eLearning portal. The eLearning sign-in uses the same theme and the same account as the main portal — one login, two destinations.

  • ●University-style home: contact strip, sticky navigation, full-width photo slider, quick-resource strip, illustrated programme levels, photo gallery, “Request free information” form on navy, studying band, review cards with star ratings, campus-news cards and an intake pop-up.
  • ●eLearning portal: the same split sign-in as Version A (“Log in to eLearning”, role tabs, admission number and password, demo credentials), then a student dashboard with photo course cards, progress bars, announcements, events and campus-life blocks, plus a “Search for courses” catalogue.
  • ●Same Eastport facts, crest and crest navy-and-gold palette throughout — photography on every screen, including the portal.

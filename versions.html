Two complete builds

Choose the design for eastportinternationalcollege.ac.ke

Both versions carry the same Eastport content, the same logo and the navy-and-gold brand palette. They differ in structure, navigation and the shape of the student login. Open each one, use the portal, then tell us which letter to take forward.

Version A

College website

A brochure-style college website in Eastport navy and gold, in the approved split / frosted-glass design: a photographic hero with a frosted enquiry card, course categories with photography, programme cards, a campus photo wall and a split student login.

  • ●Split / frosted-glass hero: the claim and intake facts on the left, a frosted “Request a place” card on the right, over a full-bleed photograph. Interior pages repeat the pattern as a title-and-facts banner.
  • ●Student login in the same language: clear copy panel with statistics and pill actions beside a frosted sign-in panel with underlined fields, pill submit and tabs for student, staff and guardian.
  • ●Ten pages: home, about, courses, course detail, news, contact, apply, login, dashboard, 404.
  • ●Campus photo wall, plus photography on the about, contact, courses and apply pages.

Version B

University website + eLearning

A university-style website with a full-width photo slider, programme levels and enquiry form, plus a separate eLearning portal with its own login, student dashboard and course catalogue.

  • ●University-style home: contact strip, sticky navigation, full-width photo slider, quick-resource strip, illustrated programme levels, photo gallery, “Request free information” form on navy, studying band, review cards with star ratings, campus-news cards and an intake pop-up.
  • ●eLearning portal: centred login card over a campus photograph (“Log in”, username or email, remember username, forgotten password, “Log in using your account on: Sign in with Eastport Email”, cookies notice), a student dashboard with photo course cards, announcements, events and campus-life blocks, plus a “Search for courses” catalogue.
  • ●Same Eastport facts, crest, navy and gold throughout — photography on every screen, including the portal.
